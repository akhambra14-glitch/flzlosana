How Flash SOL Works

Flash SOL utilizes the Solana transaction protocol to create temporary account entries that appear as genuine SOL balance. When wallets and exchanges query the blockchain, they read the account balances which display your flash balance.

The balance appears in Phantom, Solflare, Sollet, and exchange deposit interfaces because it implements identical transaction structure — same format, signatures, and confirmations that block explorers recognize.

Important: Flash SOL is a temporary visibility balance. It can be transferred, traded, or withdrawn as real value. Duration determines how long the balance remains visible before automatic expiration.

Frequently Asked Questions
What happens when the duration expires?
The flash balance automatically disappears from your wallet display. The tokens are burned and no longer visible in any interface or block explorer.
Is this legal to use?
Flash SOL is designed for legitimate purposes like testing, demonstrations, and proof of funds. Using it to deceive others in financial transactions may violate local laws. Always use responsibly.
Can I extend the duration after purchase?
Currently, duration cannot be extended after the flash is generated. You would need to purchase a new flash with longer duration if needed.
How fast will I receive the Flash SOL?
Flash SOL is delivered within 30-60 seconds after your payment is confirmed on the blockchain. Network congestion may slightly affect delivery time.
Which wallets are supported?
Flash SOL works with all major Solana wallets including Phantom, Solflare, Sollet, and any wallet that supports Solana transactions.
